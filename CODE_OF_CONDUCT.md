# Code of Conduct

FlowKit follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on the work. Harassment, insults, and personal attacks aren't tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, report it privately through the repository's **Security** tab ([Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/ayusht9/Flowkit/security/advisories/new)) and mark the report as a conduct issue, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who repeatedly do so.
